- **Camera:** locked-off bystander wide; no chasing or acknowledgment of camera. Hold one frame longer than feels comfortable before movement.
- **Audio bed:** fluorescent hum, cooler compressor cycling, and distant traffic through glass. Dialogue is caught quietly across the room; ambience does not duck.

- **Duration / place / cast:** 10s · `the-counter` · `the-regular`
- **Continuity locks:** The Regular has wet dark hair pushed back, oversized mechanic’s jacket with torn-off name patch, sleep shorts, unlaced boots, chipped black nail polish, and a face-down phone held at all times. She is never shown entering, never crosses behind the counter, and never turns the phone screen-up. Counter geography and the flickering fluorescent remain canonical.
- **Action:** The Regular is already leaning on the counter mid-sentence. Her face-down phone is beside her hand; one finger taps the laminate on each word.
- **Camera:** Locked-off wide at chest height; counter across the bottom third; flickering tube at the top edge.
- **Dialogue:** The Regular, quick and mid-thought: “So do you sell one egg.”
- **Added sound:** A fingernail taps laminate four times.
- **Last frame:** Her finger has stopped mid-tap and rests on the counter.
