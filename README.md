## The problem

AI video work often begins as a pile of prompts. Descriptions change between shots, characters drift, style instructions get lost, and revisions cannot be reproduced.

This sprint replaces that pile with a small, model-agnostic content operating system:

- a brand and visual rule set;
- canonical descriptions (“locks”) for recurring entities;
- a repeatable series format;
- structured episode and shot briefs;
- consistent prompt inheritance from brand to shot;
- machine-readable handoffs and human review sheets.

You keep an editable source of truth instead of starting over with every short.

## What you receive

For **one brand / one channel**, the sprint includes:

1. **A 90-minute intake** covering audience, voice, visual language, constraints, and content goals.
2. **A customized writers’ room** suitable for an Obsidian-style Markdown workflow.
3. **One channel system** with visual rules, voice, negative constraints, cadence, and social identity fields.
4. **A compact canon:** up to 2 recurring characters, 2 locations, and 3 props, each with reusable canonical descriptions.
5. **One repeatable series format** with a defined episode structure.
6. **3–4 compiled episodes, totaling 12–20 shots.**
7. **Per-shot production packages** containing:
   - plain-language and labeled prompts;
   - negative-prompt intent;
   - action, camera, dialogue, audio, and continuity blocks where relevant;
   - stable shot IDs and last-frame continuity notes;
   - a machine-readable JSON manifest; and
   - a human-readable contact/review sheet.
8. **One model-dialect pass** adapting the package to one generator you already use, where its documented input format permits.
9. **One consolidated revision round.**
10. **A 60-minute handoff session** showing how to edit, lint, compile, and extend the system.

Deliverables are provided as Markdown, JSON, and plain-text files in a portable project folder. The underlying workflow is model-agnostic; generator-specific behavior remains outside our control.

### 3. Booking

After written scope approval, the sprint is booked with a **20% deposit ($250)** paid in **USDC on Base**. The payment request will state the exact amount, Base network, token contract, and recipient address. Do not send funds to an address posted by an unverified third party or on another network.

### 4. Build and review

The five-business-day delivery window begins after the deposit clears and all agreed source materials are received. Work proceeds through system setup, compilation, internal checks, and delivery for review.

### 5. Acceptance and balance

Acceptance is based on the written scope—not subjective render performance. The package is accepted when it contains the agreed:

- channel and canon files;
- series format;
- 3–4 episodes and 12–20 shots;
- compiled prompt files, manifest(s), and review sheet(s);

- handoff materials/session.

You may report scope defects in one consolidated list during the acceptance window stated in the written scope. We correct valid scope defects and complete the included revision round. The **remaining 80% ($1,000)** is due in **USDC on Base** upon acceptance. Any transaction/network fee is paid by the sender unless the written scope says otherwise.

Cancellation, refund, tax, and exchange-rate treatment will be stated in the written scope before payment. Never send a deposit without that document.
